About the model

What this prototype is

A web-based decision-support prototype that estimates a low, medium or high maternal health risk category from recorded maternal and clinical values. It is the software part of the project Machine Learning-Based Maternal Health Risk Prediction: A Case Study of Mbarara Regional Referral Hospital.

It supports, and never replaces, clinical assessment and professional judgment. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment or make referral decisions.
